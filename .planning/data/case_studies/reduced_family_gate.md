Phase 7 removes no policy tag. A later phase may reduce the seven-tag mainline

## Required Future Gate Fields

| Field | Required content |
| --- | --- |
| `policy_tag` | Exact future tag proposed for removal. |
| `blocker_classification` | Must be `data issue` or `contract issue`; result quality is not allowed. |
| `blocker_evidence_location` | Source contract, matrix contract, candidate rule, or runtime incompatibility evidence. |
| `minimal_fix_attempted` | What would make the tag feasible without changing scientific intent. |
| `case_question_still_answered` | Whether the reduced family still tests the case-study question. |
| `unfavorable_baseline_protection` | Explanation proving no unfavorable baseline or competitor is selectively removed. |
| `pre_outcome_timestamp` | Timestamp proving the decision happened before profit, acceptance, opt-out, service-rate, or ranking outcomes. |

## Forbidden Removal Reasons

- A tag has lower net profit.
- A tag has worse acceptance, opt-out, service rate, or ranking.
- A tag weakens the adaptive-menu story.
- A tag is inconvenient after results are known.

## Future Unlock Conditions

Any reduced-family decision remains blocked until provenance, readiness,
artifact, and claim gates allow case execution. The reduced family must still
label semi-real geography/network, simulated demand, and simulated choice.
