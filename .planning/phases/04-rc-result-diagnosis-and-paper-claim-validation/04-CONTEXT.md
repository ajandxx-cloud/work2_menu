<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Claim 判级规则
- **D-01:** Use a strict joint-improvement threshold for any strong central
  claim. Strong evidence requires stable profit-side improvement together with
  stable service-quality improvement or preservation; profit alone is
  insufficient.
- **D-02:** If `mainline_optimized_adaptive` loses to `mainline_random_menu` on
  mean profit, Phase 4 must block universal or strong dominance language.
  Paired split behavior may still support a conditional claim when the split
  evidence is directional and clearly bounded.
- **D-03:** The observed equality between `mainline_optimized_adaptive` and
  `mainline_optimized_fixed_window` is a major diagnostic signal. Phase 4 must
  report that the adaptive-window mechanism shows no visible formal increment
  under the current formal setting unless the planner finds documented evidence
  explaining the equality.
- **D-04:** If no central strong claim is supported, Phase 4 should produce a
  dual-path conclusion: failure/reframing guidance plus a recommendation about
  whether Phase 5 calibration is mandatory or worth doing.

### Paired 统计呈现口径
- **D-05:** The primary statistical view is paired split differences:
  `mainline_optimized_adaptive - baseline` within each split, followed by
  direction and average paired difference summaries.
- **D-06:** With only five formal splits, do not report confidence intervals in
  the Phase 4 diagnosis. Report paired differences and directions instead, and
  avoid significance language.
- **D-07:** Organize metrics by claim-specific blocks rather than one universal
  ranking. Each planned claim should carry the metrics needed to support or
  reject it.
- **D-08:** Uptake regime must be reported separately for `low` and `medium`.
  If claim direction differs across regimes, downgrade the claim to
  conditional.

### Formal Run 与 Gate 使用边界
- **D-09:** Treat the latest completed 35-row formal run as formal diagnostic
  input. It may inform Phase 4 diagnosis and provisional paper-claim reasoning,
  but it is not claim-ready manuscript evidence by itself.
- **D-10:** Dirty-git provenance blocks formal claim classification. Until the
  dirty-git blocker is resolved or explicitly superseded by a clean rerun,
  Phase 4 may analyze result patterns but must mark claim classification as
  blocked or provisional diagnostic, not final.
- **D-11:** Ignore older smoke artifact and smoke claim-guard outputs when
  diagnosing Phase 4 claims. Phase 4 should focus on formal normalized rows and
  formal readiness/provenance status, not reuse smoke artifact status as the
  claim basis.
- **D-12:** Phase 4 may generate diagnostic artifacts from formal rows, such as
  paired tables, claim matrices, and provenance-status summaries. It must not
  upgrade manuscript claims or produce claim-ready artifacts while gates remain
  blocked.

### 输出文件结构与下游分叉
- **D-13:** Structure `RC_FORMAL_DIAGNOSIS.md` as blocker first, then result
  diagnostics, then claim status. The dirty-git/provenance blocker should be
  visible before any paper-claim interpretation.
- **D-14:** The claim matrix may include provisional diagnostic classifications,
  but every affected claim must be marked as blocked by provenance until the
  dirty-git issue is resolved.
- **D-15:** The Phase 5 recommendation should use a direct dual path: first
  clean up or rebuild the provenance gate, then decide whether Phase 5 is
  mandatory or whether paper reframing is the better path.
- **D-16:** Phase 4 planning should cover diagnosis, tables, and documentation
  only. It should not plan runtime behavior changes, artifact-gate rewrites, or
  claim-guard repairs as part of this phase.

### The Agent's Discretion
- The planner may choose the exact section headings and table formats for
  `RC_FORMAL_DIAGNOSIS.md`, provided D-01 through D-16 are represented.
- The planner may decide whether diagnostic artifacts are generated by
  existing scripts, ad hoc read-only analysis, or Markdown tables, as long as
  formal rows are not edited and runtime behavior is not changed.
- The planner may decide the exact wording of provisional claim labels, but
  final claim classification must remain blocked while dirty-git provenance is
  unresolved.
