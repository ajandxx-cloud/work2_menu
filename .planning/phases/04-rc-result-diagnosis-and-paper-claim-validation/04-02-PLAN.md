# Phase 4 Plan 2: Claim Matrix And Paper-Claim Diagnosis

<objective>
Write `.planning/results/RC_FORMAL_DIAGNOSIS.md` as the authoritative Phase 4
diagnosis: blocker first, formal result diagnostics second, claim matrix third,
and downstream Phase 5 routing last.
</objective>

<scope>
## In Scope

- Classify each planned paper claim as strong, conditional, weak/diagnostic, or
  unsupported, while marking all empirical claim classifications as
  provisional/blocked when provenance gates remain blocked.
- Explain profit, cost, acceptance, served rate, opt-out, home share,
  meeting-point uptake, product ablations, fixed/adaptive window behavior,
  split direction, and uptake-regime behavior.
- Produce failure/reframing guidance when strong central claims are not
  supported.
- Recommend whether Phase 5 calibration is mandatory, worth doing, or eligible
  for skipped-by-gate after provenance cleanup.
- Update planning state only to reflect the completed diagnosis and next-route
  recommendation.

## Out Of Scope

- Repairing artifact gates or claim guard code.
- Cleaning dirty git automatically.
- Rerunning formal replay, calibration, sensitivity, or case-study work.
- Editing manuscript claims, generated result rows, generated tables, or
  generated figures.
</scope>

## Task 2: Classify Planned Claims

**Type:** docs
**Files:** `.planning/results/RC_FORMAL_DIAGNOSIS.md`,
`.planning/paper/TR_E_RESEARCH_DESIGN.md`

**Action:**

Use the Phase 2 claim ladder and Plan 1 diagnostic tables to classify at least:

1. Central optimized adaptive `m+w+p` superiority versus all six baselines.
2. Product ablation value: adaptive versus `m` and `m+w`.
3. Window ablation value: adaptive versus fixed-window optimized menu.
4. Menu construction value: optimized menu versus no-menu, fixed-menu, and
   random-menu baselines.
5. Provenance/status claim: what can be said about reproducibility and gates.

For each claim, include:

- claim ID;
- comparison;
- required metrics;
- observed evidence;
- paired direction summary;
- uptake-regime caveat;
- classification;
- blocker status;
- allowed manuscript use.

Apply these rules:

- If adaptive loses to random menu on mean net profit, universal/strong
  dominance is unsupported or blocked.
- If adaptive equals fixed-window, window-increment claims are unsupported or
  weak/diagnostic.
- If profit improves but service quality is mixed or regime-dependent, classify
  as conditional or weak/diagnostic.
- All empirical positive classifications remain blocked/provisional until
  dirty-git and artifact gates are resolved.

**Verify:**

- `CLAIM-02` is covered by one claim matrix row per planned claim.
- `CLAIM-03` is covered by explicit unsupported/mixed-result routing.
- `CLAIM-05` is covered by conditional-study reframing when strong dominance
  is not supported.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- The claim matrix tells downstream phases exactly what can be written, what is
  blocked, and what must be reframed.

## Task 3: Write Phase 5 Routing And Reframing Guidance

**Type:** docs
**Files:** `.planning/results/RC_FORMAL_DIAGNOSIS.md`,
`.planning/PROJECT.md`,
`.planning/ROADMAP.md`

**Action:**

Add a downstream routing section that recommends one of:

1. Clean or rebuild the provenance gate, then rerun claim-ready artifact gates.
2. Execute Phase 5 calibration because the central claim is weak, unstable, or
   unsupported.
3. Skip Phase 5 by gate only if at least one central claim has stable paired
   differences after provenance cleanup.
4. Reframe the paper as a conditional service-menu design study if no strong
   central claim is defensible.

If the diagnosis finds no central strong claim, include failure diagnosis and
reframing guidance before downstream expansion into case study or sensitivity.

Update `.planning/PROJECT.md` only if the project-level current status or key
decision needs to record the Phase 4 outcome. Update `.planning/ROADMAP.md`
only if Phase 5 should be explicitly marked mandatory or skipped-by-gate. Do
not mark Phase 5 skipped while provenance remains blocked.

**Verify:**

- The recommendation does not tune on formal results or ask for hand-edited
  rows.
- Phase 5 is not skipped unless the roadmap gate is actually satisfied.
- The fallback contribution path remains available.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- The project has a clear next action after Phase 4, not just a table dump.
