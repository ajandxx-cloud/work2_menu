# Plan 03 - Final Claim-Safety And Traceability Verification

<objective>
Verify that the Phase 5 manuscript package satisfies all manuscript
requirements, source-map contracts, and prohibited-language constraints before
Phase 6 readiness audit. This plan updates only audit/verification companion
files unless it finds manuscript body issues that must be corrected.
</objective>

<scope>
In scope:
- Run script-style claim-guard and smoke checks.
- Verify all required manuscript deliverables exist.
- Verify required TR-E sections exist.
- Scan the manuscript body for prohibited positive language.
- Confirm C1 through C8 claim audit coverage.
- Confirm table/figure source map has required traceability columns.
- Update audit files and internal-review response with final verification
  results and unresolved Phase 6 risks.

Out of scope:
- Regenerating evidence, package status, package index, claim guards, or root
  mirrors.
- Running final replay, calibration, checkpoint training, case-study execution,
  or artifact builders.
- Expanding the manuscript into final LaTeX submission form.
</scope>

<must_haves>
<truths>
- D-08: The manuscript says positive empirical claims remain blocked.
- D-10: Results open with claim-gate/status table.
- D-11: Phase 8 no-filter remains diagnostic boundary only.
- D-12: Phase 9 exact/greedy remains appendix-first and blocked for credibility claims.
- D-13: Case scaffold remains scaffold/future-work only.
- D-14: Every table/figure has full source and claim metadata.
- D-16: Near-optimality or greedy optimality language is absent.
</truths>
</must_haves>
